Academic Engineering Projects

Payload Recovery System for a Sounding Rocket

Special Engineering Project · University of Los Andes · 2019

Designed a conceptual payload-recovery system for a sounding rocket with an intended apogee of approximately 100 kilometers.

The project included benchmarking of recovery technologies, parachute and ejection-system design, instrumentation selection, flight and dispersion simulations, manufacturing-cost estimation, assembly planning, prototype-component development, and technical drawings.

Technical areas: Aerospace engineering, mechanical design, parachute systems, simulation, instrumentation, technical research, and cost evaluation.

Wind-Powered Water Pumping for Agricultural Applications

Bachelor’s Thesis · University of Los Andes · 2020

Researched and developed a conceptual wind-powered water-pumping system for agricultural irrigation.

The project combined environmental-data analysis, wind-resource modelling, hydraulic requirements, commercial equipment comparison, theoretical performance modelling, cost evaluation, rotor-sizing considerations, and irrigation-system selection.

The study evaluated conditions for onion cultivation in Samacá, Colombia, and compared wind-powered pumping with solar and conventional alternatives. It also investigated rotor modifications to improve pumping performance and the use of drip irrigation to improve water efficiency.

Technical areas: Renewable energy, wind engineering, mechanical design, fluid systems, environmental-data analysis, agricultural irrigation, and techno-economic evaluation.
