The project

The Adapted Modular Scimitar is a multidisciplinary UAV project involving the adaptation and integration of a modular unmanned-aircraft platform.

Engineering areas

  • Mechanical and modular airframe design
  • CAD and manufacture-ready geometry
  • Embedded sensing and electronics
  • Flight-system configuration and integration
  • Prototype assembly and validation
