Adapted Modular Scimitar UAV

An open-source, 3D-printed fixed-wing UAV adapted for modular environmental sensing, payload integration, and field testing.

Fixed-wing UAV Embedded systems PX4 Team project

The project

The project adapted the open-source Scimitar V2 airframe into a modular platform for airborne environmental sensing.

The aircraft was manufactured using 3D-printed components and modified to support interchangeable sensor payloads. The work combined mechanical adaptation, electronics integration, aerodynamic analysis, firmware configuration, and field testing.

System development

  • Adaptation of an open-source fixed-wing UAV platform
  • Development of a reconfigurable payload mounting grid
  • Integration of environmental sensors and flight electronics
  • PX4 flight-controller and QGroundControl configuration
  • Aerodynamic screening before manufacturing
  • Assembly, hand-launch testing, and sensor-data validation

My contribution

This was a collaborative project. My contribution focused on the software, flight-system configuration, system analysis, and electronics integration.

  • Configured PX4 firmware and aircraft parameters
  • Used QGroundControl for flight-system setup and mission planning
  • Contributed to software and hardware selection
  • Supported the mounting and integration of onboard electronics
  • Contributed to simulation, formal analysis, and system testing
  • Contributed to technical documentation and manuscript writing

Key results

  • Developed a sub-1.5 kg open-source fixed-wing UAV platform
  • Created a modular mounting grid for interchangeable sensor payloads
  • Used simulation-based screening to evaluate the aerodynamic effects of payload integration
  • Improved the estimated lift-to-drag ratio of the payload-grid design from approximately 3.6–5.1 to 8.8–18.2
  • Completed field testing of the aircraft and onboard sensor system
  • Demonstrated in-flight temperature, humidity, and barometric pressure measurements
