Hyperspectral Plastic Identification

A machine-learning workflow for identifying plastic materials from spectral signatures and hyperspectral measurements.

Machine learningHyperspectral imagingPythonMaterial identification

The problem

Different materials can appear visually similar while having distinct spectral responses. Hyperspectral measurements provide richer information than conventional RGB images, but they also require careful preprocessing and modelling.

The approach

Spectral data
Preprocessing
Feature reduction
Classification
Material prediction

The project uses public reference data together with measurements from the NEWTEC BUTEO system to explore plastic identification across different spectral sources.

My contribution

  • Prepared and explored hyperspectral datasets.
  • Developed the preprocessing and machine-learning workflow.
  • Compared material spectra and classification behaviour.
  • Structured the experiments and documented limitations across measurement systems.

What this project demonstrates

Scientific machine learning, spectral-data processing, dimensionality reduction, classification, experimental comparison, and clear communication of model limitations.
