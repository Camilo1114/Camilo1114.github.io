Independent project

Animal Species Classification from Audio

A comparison of deep variational autoencoder and convolutional approaches for classifying animal species from environmental audio recordings.

Deep learningAudio processingPyTorchSignal processing

The problem

Environmental audio recordings contain background noise, variable call duration, and overlapping frequency patterns. The project investigates how learned representations and direct convolutional classification perform on this type of data.

The approach

Audio recordings
MFCC features
CNN / DVAE
Classifier
Species prediction

The study compares a convolutional approach with a deep variational autoencoder representation followed by classification.

My contribution

  • Prepared the environmental audio data and acoustic features.
  • Implemented and evaluated convolutional and variational approaches.
  • Compared model behaviour and generalization.
  • Documented the experimental workflow and results.

What this project demonstrates

Audio signal processing, deep learning, representation learning, experimental comparison, and model evaluation.
